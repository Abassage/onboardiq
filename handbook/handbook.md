# Northwind Traders — Employee Handbook (SAMPLE - fictional company, for demo use)

## Welcome
Welcome to Northwind Traders! Your first week is about settling in. Your manager will walk you through your 30-60-90 day plan, and your onboarding buddy — a teammate assigned before your start date — is your go-to for everyday questions.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid time off per year, accrued monthly. PTO requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Unused PTO up to 5 days rolls over into the next calendar year.

## Holidays
Northwind observes 10 paid holidays per year, including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (2 days), and Christmas (2 days). The full holiday calendar is posted in the HR portal each December.

## Benefits
Health, dental, and vision coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date. Northwind covers 80% of employee premiums. Open enrollment runs each November. Questions about plans go to benefits@northwind.example.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. A $500 home-office stipend is provided in your first paycheck. You must be available during core hours, 10am–3pm Central, on remote days.

## IT Setup
Your laptop ships before your start date. On day one, follow the setup guide taped inside the box to enroll in device management, then install Okta Verify to access company apps. For help, contact the IT helpdesk in the #it-help Slack channel.

## Expenses
Business expenses under $75 need only a receipt uploaded to the expenses portal. Anything over $75 requires manager approval before purchase. Expense reports are due by the 5th of each month for the prior month's spending.

## Code of Conduct
Treat colleagues with respect, protect confidential company information, and report concerns to your manager or HR. Northwind has zero tolerance for harassment of any kind.
